# BrightSmile Dental - Clinic Details & FAQs

## Services & Rates
- **New Patient Special**: $79 flat package includes comprehensive cleaning, full exam, and digital X-rays.
- **Routine Cleaning**: $150 for existing patients without insurance.
- **Teeth Whitening**: In-office cosmetic whitening for $299 (usually $500).
- **Emergency Examination**: $45 exam fee. Daily slots are held open for emergency dental pain.

## Insurance Policies
- **Accepted**: Major dental PPO insurance plans (Delta Dental, Aetna, Cigna, MetLife, Guardian).
- **Not Accepted**: HMO plans, Medi-Cal, or Medicaid.
