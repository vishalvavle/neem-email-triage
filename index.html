Frequently Asked Questions

Clinical Compliance & Implementation FAQ

Clear answers on HIPAA security rules, psychiatric triage protocols, EHR integration, and deployment timelines.

Is NeemTechAI fully HIPAA compliant and do you sign a BAA?

Yes, 100%. NeemTechAI executes mutual Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with all healthcare organizations, psychiatric centers, and private practices prior to live deployment. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and all stored data is secured with AES-256 military-grade encryption.

Furthermore, our AI employees run within zero-retention private sandboxes—patient interactions and ePHI are never stored for AI model training or shared with external third parties.

How does the AI employee handle psychiatric evaluations and crisis situations?

For psychiatric and behavioral health practices, our AI conducts compassionate, clinical intake tailored to 42 CFR Part 2 and state privacy laws. It gathers non-emergent patient history, preferred therapy modalities, and insurance information.

Crucially, if a patient conveys acute distress, suicidal ideation, or thoughts of self-harm, the AI immediately activates hardcoded clinical safety guardrails: it provides the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and 911 contact info on-screen, halts conversational questioning, and instantly sends an emergency SMS/email alert to your practice's on-call clinical triage team.

Which EHR and Practice Management Systems do you integrate with?

We provide bi-directional synchronization with leading Electronic Health Record (EHR) and Practice Management Systems (PMS). Supported platforms include Epic Systems, Athenahealth, Kareo, Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Calendly, and secure FHIR/HL7 clinical APIs.

Confirmed appointment times, intake notes, and demographic questionnaires flow directly into your existing provider schedules without requiring staff to re-enter data.

How does this differ from generic website chatbots or web forms?

Generic chatbots and basic contact forms have low conversion rates and present catastrophic regulatory vulnerabilities because they lack HIPAA BAA guarantees and medical logic. NeemTechAI operates as an autonomous digital employee trained specifically on your clinic's scheduling rules, credentialed providers, insurance plans, and clinical escalation criteria.

It responds in under 1 second 24/7, answers detailed practice questions, verifies insurance coverage, and secures confirmed appointments while patients are actively motivated.

What is the onboarding and deployment timeline?

Our turnkey deployment typically takes 5 to 7 business days. Our engineers ingest your practice FAQs and provider schedules, execute your custom BAA, configure your crisis guardrails, and conduct extensive simulated testing before connecting your practice phone, SMS, and website channels.
